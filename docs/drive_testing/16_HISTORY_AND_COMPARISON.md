# History and Comparison

History lists configuration, host metrics, repeats, failures, external observation, QC, and notes. Local deletion requires confirmation and does not delete third-party driving history.

Comparison presets include speed thresholds, distance thresholds, update intervals, constant versus acceleration/city, city versus highway, timed updates versus GPX, phone movement state, and observed outcome categories.

All outcome summaries display:

```text
Exploratory observations only. Not a validated success probability.
```

Fewer than three comparable completed runs are shown as raw counts, not percentages.
