# Safety and Ethics

- Use only devices and test accounts owned by or authorized for the tester.
- Do not falsify another person's safety records.
- Do not automate or modify Life360, Arity, or another third-party application.
- Do not bypass simulated-location detection, modify third-party binaries, intercept private traffic, scrape accounts, or conceal simulation.
- Do not use unrealistic negative controls as recommendations.
- A passenger should operate the computer and phone controls when a vehicle is moving.
- Stop the test if device, tunnel, or location writes fail.
- Record external outcomes honestly and manually.
