# Quick Test

1. Launch in `drive-testing` mode.
2. Initialize the device and pass readiness checks.
3. Stop stable Drive Mode if it is active.
4. Generate or reuse a valid OSRM road route at least 0.6 mile long.
5. Open Live Test and click **Run Quick Test**.
6. Review the calculated duration for 20 mph, 0.6 mile, one-second updates.
7. Confirm authorized use and start.
8. Watch live host-side progress and write telemetry.
9. Let the controller finish or stop it cleanly.
10. Verify the Reset GPS result when selected.
11. Record the external observation manually.
12. Generate and inspect the report and QC status.

Quick Test never marks an external result successful automatically.
